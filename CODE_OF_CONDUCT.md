# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Critique code, not the person who wrote it.

## What's expected

- Give feedback that is specific and actionable. "This breaks when the CSV has a blank
  header" is useful; "this is bad code" is not.
- Accept feedback the same way you'd want to give it.
- Remember that contributors have different levels of experience, first languages, and
  amounts of free time. A question that seems basic to you took someone courage to ask.

## What's not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing someone's private
information, or persistent disruption after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

Report anything that crosses the line to **abdul.haseeb4924@gmail.com**. Reports stay
confidential.

The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete comments and other contributions that don't
follow this document, and may ban anyone for behaviour they consider inappropriate.
